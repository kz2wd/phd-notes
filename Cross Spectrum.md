[[Covariance]] in spectral space

$$S_{pu}(k)=\langle \overline{\hat{p}(k)} . \hat{u}(k)\rangle - \overline{\langle \hat{p}(k)\rangle} . \langle \hat{u}(k) \rangle$$
Indicates how much two signal are linked, per Fourier mode k.
The two signals must be sampled over time (or another axis) for this to be meaningful.
