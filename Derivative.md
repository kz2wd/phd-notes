Operator returning the rate of change of a function or a field.

Can be partial; only along one direction.

[[Abstract Representation]]:
non-continuous operator applicable on differentiable field (need Space to b at least C1).
Be careful as the continuity of the operator is not the same as the one of the field.

There are many [[Implementation]]s, with different [[Boundary Condition]]s:
- Explicit schemes
- Implicit, [[Compact differentiation]]
- [[Spectral differentiation]]
