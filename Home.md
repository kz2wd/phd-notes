This project is a mental map of all the concepts related to my researches.

The goal is to externalize and reference the mental construction behind the various models required.

The main theme of this research is: [[velocity]] reconstruction from wall-[[pressure]] in the context of wall-bounded channels, with friction Reynolds number 200 to 1000.

The goal is to produce a prediction pipeline using a [[diffusion framework]] restrained by [[Navier-Stokes]] based losses.
